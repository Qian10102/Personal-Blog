The most useful interface is rarely the loudest one.

## Start with rhythm

Good reading experiences give each element enough room to breathe. Type scale, line length, and vertical spacing are not decoration; they are part of the interface.

## Use contrast deliberately

One strong focal point is usually more useful than a page full of competing accents.
